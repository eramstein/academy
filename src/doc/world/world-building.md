# Academy World Design

Setting for the game and justification for the card game.

## The World

A low fantasy version of our own world, in the early 19th century, with steam punk technology.

The Academy is located in the Black Forest (western germany), but attracts students from all over Europe, the Arabic world, and even sometimes from African and Asian countries.

## The Magic and the Cards

In this fantasy world, there is no magic wands and incantations, magic is fully linked to crafting and engineering. It is low fantsay, no dragon or spells. People have discovered a way to capture essences into small objects, and re-use these objects in every-day life. Cards have emerged as the most practical vessel: portable, conductive to magic essences, convenient to craft and trade. These cards can become sentient and affect the real world in more or less powerful ways. They can be used as advisors, seers, construction tools, and even defensive mechanisms by summoning temporary physical manifestations of their essence. It should be constrained by the low fantasy setting though, no world-bending effects, it is everyday life convenience effects most iften.

There are 4 essential essences, which can be combined into a single card.
Green is nature, growth and health.
Blue is wisdom, knowledge, illusio nand trickery.
Red is chaos, speed and aggression.
Black is solidity, industry, organisatio nand decay.

Different cultures enjoy specific combinations. For example, Italian city states are fond of blue/black cards, Arabic lands commonly use red/blue cards, etc... Each 2 color combinations has an associated culture which reflects in the theme of the card.

## Artificery

The rare people who have the skills and magical aura to craft cards are named artificers. It is a very prestigious and coveted profession. Magic cards are rare and very valuable. It takes many years before being able to finally create practically useful and sentient cards.

## The Academy

Various schools across the world train artificers. The training usually starts arounf 20/25 years old, as it requires a mature magical aura. There is an age old rivalry between these schools, who try to attract the best students, but also the best patrons and guilds partnerships.

The game takes place at The Academy, one of the oldest schools in Europe. Its prestige lies more in its ancient history than recent successes. Newer and better funded schools have emerged. Nevertheless, the Academy keeps a unique reputation that can attract some of the most unusual - and talented - students.

## The Teaching of Artificery

An old and widespread tradition for teaching youg artificers is to have them create playing cards. Schools hgave agreed on a specific game, named "Hordes" **(note: find a better name...)** , a complex startegic game mixing cards and chess like elements.

These cards usually have very low magical properties, especially with beginner students. They are merely "normal" playing cards that follow the rules of the game by themselves. For example, a 5/5 dragon card will not actually summon a big flying dragon. It will be a simple piece of cardboard illustrating a dragon, and when played on the board will properly follow the game rules of what this card mechanics do. Imagine the card moving on its own on the board, properly taking track of numbers, following its owner orders, and respecting the complex rules properly.

Cards have colors connected to the different essences of magic, and keywords and abilities that require mastering artificery skills to be implenmented properly. For example, a student with an affinity for blue cards and control abilities might later become a famous artificer for customers looking for magical advisors.

While maybe not impressive, it is already extremely hard to produce such a card. Creating these cards is a way for schools to teach technical crafting skills in a controlled environment, and a way to compare the students performance in a standardized way. In addition, the actual game being very strategic, it tests not only the crafting skills of the prospects, but also their startegic accumen, a very valuable quality for an artificer.

## Sentient Cards

As the students progress, they might eventually manage to create their first sentient card. These are the type of cards that artificers are actually being paid to produce in the real world. They have a mind of their own, and can affect the real world, even though in that case they were made merely for a pedagogic game. It is the dream and holy grail for students to be able to create these cards.
