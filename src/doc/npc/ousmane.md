# Back story

Ousmane Tempeli comes from a distant African empire. He is twenty-nine now, a former soldier, and he has set himself a different campaign: to become a great artificer, as his grandfather Omar was, a name still spoken with awe in the trade of artifact crafting. An old traume from war still haunts him from time to time. Another worry is not to live up to his grand father's legend as an artificer, even though he's highly competent already.

# Personality

Ousmane is very energetic, has a bright and jovial personlity, except for rare moments of melancholy triggered by war trauma. He's very charismatic and enjoy flirting with women, even though he's not interested in a relationship for now, fully focused on his studies. Behind his easy going style is hidden a lot of ambition, and a desire to be worthy of his glorious ancestor.

# Relation to other characters

Ousmane sees Antonio (the player character) like a smaller brother. He's quite protective but also likes to tease him, specifically when it comes to women, a domain in which Antonio is a lot less confortable and experimented than Ousmane. He sometimes does a tag team with Emma, especially if Antonio happens to be smitten by her. He also tries to help Antonio get more confidence.

Ousmane can also be a great partner for crafting artifacts, a domain in which he excels.
