A possible narrative structure
I'd propose organizing Emma's events around three overlapping themes rather than three separate friendship, romance, and rivalry paths.
Emma's personal journey
Ambition, achievement, financial security, family pride

Collaboration
Shared goals, trust, friendship

Competition
Rivalry, admiration, challenge

Intimacy
Attraction, vulnerability, commitment

Possible outcomes
Trusted teammate · Business partner · Respected rival · Romantic partner · Spouse

The crucial idea is that Emma's personal ambitions continue evolving regardless of whether the player pursues her romantically, befriends her, or competes against her.
Her story isn't exclusively about her relationship with the player.
One suggestion: give Emma a genuine dilemma
Her backstory offers a particularly promising source of dramatic tension.
What happens when her ambitions conflict with her personal attachments?
For example, late in the campaign, Emma might receive an exceptional opportunity to work with a prestigious artificer in another city. It could secure her financial future, but require leaving the Academy and the people she's grown close to.
Depending on the player's relationship with her, this could become:

- A friendship event where you help her evaluate the opportunity.
- A rivalry event where you compete for the same prestigious position.
- A romantic event where you must discuss what a shared future would actually look like.
  The central dilemma remains the same, but the player's involvement changes substantially.
  And importantly, Emma should retain her own agency. She shouldn't automatically abandon a lifelong ambition because the player's love value is high enough.
  That would make her feel more like a believable character.
