1. The Art of Conversation
   Comedy · Early game · Courtyard or cafeteria

Ousmane notices Antonio looking rather uncomfortable around an attractive student. He decides that his young friend urgently needs lessons in the art of charming women.

He demonstrates with effortless success, then insists Antonio try. Unfortunately, his chosen subject is Emma, who immediately recognizes what's going on.

"Are you teaching him to flirt, Ousmane? How charitable. And how very optimistic."

Choices: Antonio can play along, attempt to impress Emma, or turn the tables by deliberately embarrassing his instructor.

Consequences: Friendship with Ousmane, possible Emma friendship/rivalry, and a recurring joke about Antonio's romantic education. Special dialogue if emma_smitten exists.

2. The Unbreakable Teapot
   Slapstick · Early game · Academy workshop

Ousmane proudly presents his latest creation: an enchanted teapot guaranteed to withstand any impact. He invites Antonio to test it.

The teapot is indeed indestructible. Unfortunately, the table is not. Neither is the floor. Nor, as it turns out, the ceiling of the room below.

Professor Lombard would very much like an explanation.

Choices: Help Ousmane fix the damage, improve the enchantment, or suggest an entirely new commercial application for an indestructible projectile.

Consequences: Crafting experience, artifact resources, or an amusing new card. Potential recurring appearances of the teapot. 3. A Gentleman Never Pays Full Price
Social comedy · Early game · Town materials shop

Ousmane takes Antonio shopping for artificery supplies and discovers that the merchant is charging an outrageous price for a particular component.

What follows is a magnificent display of negotiation. Ousmane compliments the merchant's intelligence, praises his family, discusses international commerce, and somehow manages to get invited for tea.

Forty minutes later, Antonio is still standing there holding an empty shopping bag.

Choices: Join the negotiations with a Charisma check, discreetly research the item's actual value with Intelligence, or simply pay the price and escape.

Consequences: Potential discount, gold savings, or a new contact at the shop. Ousmane can teach Antonio a thing or two about persuasion. 4. A Tactical Assessment
Comedy · Early game · Cafeteria

Ousmane watches Antonio play Hordes and announces that his tactics are in desperate need of military discipline.

He begins explaining the principles of warfare, complete with improvised formations made from bread rolls, cheese, and cutlery.

The lesson becomes increasingly elaborate until the cafeteria staff intervenes. Apparently, the cheese cavalry has invaded somebody else's lunch.

Choices: Challenge his tactical theory, embrace the absurdity and participate, or propose an actual Hordes match to settle the debate.

Consequences: TCG match, strategic knowledge, rivalry or friendship. Could unlock occasional tactical discussions with Ousmane. 5. The Most Dangerous Man at the Academy
Social comedy · Early/mid game · Royal Pigeon Inn

Ousmane invites Antonio for an evening at the inn. He is soon entertaining half the common room with stories, jokes, and flirtatious banter.

Then a rather imposing fellow challenges him to a game of Hordes, apparently hoping to impress a young woman who has been laughing at Ousmane's stories.

Ousmane accepts graciously, then whispers to Antonio: "My young friend, I have a terrible problem. I promised myself I wouldn't humiliate anyone tonight."

Choices: Offer tactical advice, encourage Ousmane to play seriously, or suggest that Antonio take his place.

Consequences: Match opportunity, gold wager, or reputation. This could establish Ousmane as a popular figure in town. 6. The Grandson of Omar
Character development · Mid game · Library

Antonio discovers Ousmane studying an old artificery treatise written by his grandfather, Omar Tempeli.

Ousmane is unusually quiet. He explains that his grandfather's inventions remain classics, studied and reproduced across the world.

He has been trying to recreate one particularly difficult device, but every attempt has fallen short. He laughs about it, though without his usual enthusiasm.

"Imagine spending your life trying to catch up with a man who's had decades of a head start. Very inconsiderate of him, really."

Choices: Offer technical assistance, suggest that Ousmane develop his own design instead, or challenge him to demonstrate what he has achieved already.

Consequences: Respect, friendship, and the beginning of a crafting mini-arc. Antonio might learn an advanced artifact pattern. 7. A Perfectly Safe Expedition
Adventure comedy · Mid game · Forest of Schlupak

Ousmane has discovered that a rare crafting ingredient can be found somewhere in the forest. He proposes a short excursion.

He assures Antonio that his military experience makes the journey entirely safe.

Naturally, they get lost.

Worse, Ousmane refuses to admit it. According to him, they're conducting a reconnaissance operation in unfamiliar territory.

The situation escalates when they discover evidence that somebody else has been using the abandoned ruins nearby.

Choices: Follow Ousmane's instincts, use Intelligence to navigate, or investigate the ruins.

Consequences: Rare ingredients, a possible hidden location, and a new memory with Ousmane. Could lead to a second, more serious adventure. 8. The Night of a Thousand Compliments
Comedy · Mid game · Academy gathering

Ousmane makes a wager with Antonio: he claims he can make any person at the Academy smile with a sincere compliment.

Antonio gets to choose his target.

Potential victims include Emma, Molly, Elsa, Farid, and Professor Glützenbaum.

Most encounters go surprisingly well. Then Antonio chooses Elsa.

For once, Ousmane discovers that charm may not be a universally applicable solution.

Choices: Select the target, help Ousmane recover from an awkward situation, or double the wager.

Consequences: Small wagers, character-specific interactions and amusing callbacks. Particularly good for weaving Ousmane into other NPCs' stories. 9. Two Hammers, One Genius
Crafting · Mid game · Academy workshop

Ousmane and Frank are collaborating on an ambitious artificery project. Both are excellent craftsmen. Both are convinced their approach is correct.

Unfortunately, they disagree about nearly everything.

Antonio is invited to act as an impartial judge, an appointment which quickly proves to involve considerably more physical labor than anticipated.

Choices: Support Ousmane's elegant but complex design, Frank's robust and practical solution, or propose a hybrid approach requiring a difficult crafting check.

Consequences: Artifact pattern, skills, friendship or respect with both characters. A hybrid success could create a unique tool for crafting. 10. The Sound of Thunder
Serious · Mid/late game · Workshop or courtyard

An experimental device backfires with a tremendous bang.

Everyone laughs about the accident, except Ousmane. For a brief moment, he seems somewhere else entirely.

He recovers quickly and dismisses the incident with a joke, but Antonio has noticed.

Later, if they have developed sufficient trust, Ousmane speaks a little about his years as a soldier. Not a dramatic confession, just a few memories of people he knew, a decision he regrets, and the strange experience of returning to peaceful civilian life.

Choices: Listen without pressing him, share a personal experience, or offer to leave the subject alone.

Consequences: Friendship and a character-sheet discovery. It might open later, quieter conversations.

I would keep this event genuinely serious. The humor belongs in Ousmane's ordinary life, not in his trauma. 11. The Rescue of Professor Glützenbaum
Adventure comedy · Mid/late game · Forest

A professor conducting field research has failed to return from the forest. Ousmane organizes a small rescue party and immediately slips into his old military habits.

He assigns roles, establishes a marching order, and insists upon strict discipline.

Antonio finds himself promoted to second-in-command. This sounds rather prestigious until he learns that his responsibilities include carrying all the equipment.

The expedition eventually discovers that the professor is in genuine trouble, perhaps trapped by an old artificery mechanism or an unstable bridge.

Choices: Plan a cautious rescue with Ousmane, improvise a clever solution, or attempt a daring intervention.

Consequences: Adventure rewards, a rare card or component, and substantial respect. We see Ousmane's competence under pressure, in contrast with his usual jovial manner. 12. An Inconvenient Reputation
Character development · Mid/late game · Town

A wealthy patron visiting the Academy recognizes the Tempeli name and offers Ousmane a lucrative commission.

The catch: he doesn't particularly care about Ousmane's work. He wants a faithful reproduction of one of Omar's famous designs, complete with the family signature.

Ousmane could use the money and the prestige. But he's rather tired of being introduced as somebody's grandson.

Choices: Encourage him to accept the commission, propose a new design that improves upon the original, or help negotiate a more ambitious contract.

Consequences: Different crafting opportunities and rewards, plus progress toward Ousmane's professional independence.

This event should acknowledge his dilemma without making the financially pragmatic option inherently wrong. 13. The Unlikely Bodyguard
Feel-good comedy · Mid game · Town

Antonio encounters Ousmane accompanying a nervous first-year student to a difficult meeting with a merchant or official.

It turns out Ousmane has quietly been helping several younger students navigate Academy life.

He insists this is nothing special.

Antonio now has the opportunity to watch Ousmane being almost embarrassingly paternal, including unsolicited advice about posture, confidence, breakfast, and suitable footwear.

Choices: Help the student directly, tease Ousmane about his collection of adopted younger siblings, or suggest a more organized mentorship effort.

Consequences: Friendship, goodwill with other students, perhaps a recurring activity. It shows that Ousmane's protectiveness extends beyond Antonio. 14. The Masterpiece
Crafting climax · Late game · Academy workshop

Ousmane has finally developed an original artifact worthy of his ambitions. It combines his grandfather's methods with techniques inspired by his own experience and his time at the Academy.

He asks Antonio to help with the final assembly.

The work is extremely difficult. Something goes wrong during the testing phase, forcing them to decide whether to salvage the current prototype or start again with a more daring design.

Choices: Pursue reliability, take a calculated risk for greater power, or sacrifice some of Antonio's own rare materials to improve the result.

Consequences: A unique powerful artifact, significant crafting knowledge, or a permanent collaboration opportunity.

The emotional climax shouldn't depend solely on technical perfection. Ousmane's achievement is that he's finally producing work that belongs to him. 15. To Absent Friends
Bittersweet, feel-good · Late game · Royal Pigeon Inn

After an important achievement, Ousmane invites Antonio for a celebratory drink.

The conversation wanders through the Academy, their earlier adventures, and the extraordinary number of disasters Antonio has survived under Ousmane's supervision.

Eventually Ousmane speaks about old comrades and friends who never had the opportunity to pursue new lives.

He raises his glass.

"To absent friends. And to those of us still fortunate enough to make complete fools of ourselves."

Antonio can respond seriously, return the humor, or simply share the silence.

Consequences: A meaningful relationship milestone, with callbacks to their shared adventures.

This would be a lovely moment to establish Ousmane as a lifelong friend, without making the relationship depend on any particular romantic or professional outcome elsewhere in the game.
