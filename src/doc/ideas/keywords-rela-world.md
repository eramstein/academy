Card knowledge could become directly relevant to RPG checks.
Imagine Antonio and Emma encounter an ancient door with a damaged magical locking mechanism.
The available options might depend on what Antonio knows:

- Knowledge of armor or fortifyLand: reinforce the mechanism to prevent it collapsing.
- Knowledge of destroyUnit or decay effects: carefully weaken the seal.
- Knowledge of movement or spatial manipulation: attempt to shift the locking components.
- General Intelligence check: examine the mechanism without specialized knowledge.
  We wouldn't need an elaborate new RPG magic system. We could reuse existing knowledge flags from the TCG.
