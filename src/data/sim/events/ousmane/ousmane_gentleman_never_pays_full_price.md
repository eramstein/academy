# Event: A Gentleman Never Pays Full Price

**Event ID:** `ousmane_gentleman_never_pays_full_price`

**Trigger:** Day ≥ 5, evening.

**Invitation:** Ousmane is examining some expensive artificery supplies at the materials shop.

**Place** materials_shop

## Text

You find Ousmane at the materials shop, engaged in what appears to be a remarkably friendly conversation with the merchant. Both men are laughing heartily, although the merchant's smile becomes noticeably more strained whenever Ousmane glances at the price tags.

"Antonio! Excellent timing, my young friend. Come, come! Our esteemed merchant was just explaining why a handful of moxes costs more than a respectable horse."

"Quality, sir," the merchant insists. "Finest quality. Imported at considerable expense."

"Naturally! And I would never insult such a distinguished establishment by paying the advertised price."

Ousmane turns to you with a conspiratorial grin. "You see, Antonio, negotiation is an art. A dance between gentlemen. And like any dance, one must know when to lead."

You have a suspicion that the merchant would prefer to sit this particular dance out.

"Now, watch closely. You might learn something useful."

## Options

### Option A: Let the master demonstrate

You decide to observe Ousmane's technique. There are, after all, worse ways to spend an afternoon than watching somebody else argue about money.

Ousmane launches into a magnificent performance. He praises the merchant's intelligence, his impeccable taste, and the extraordinary reputation of his establishment. He inquires after his family, discusses the international trade in magical ingredients, and somehow manages to get invited for tea.

Forty minutes later, the merchant is laughing like an old friend. He has also agreed to sell the supplies at a fraction of their original price.

"Observe, Antonio," Ousmane whispers as you leave. "The secret is to make the other fellow feel he's won."

"Did he?"

"Of course not! But he feels magnificent, and that is nearly as good."

You spend the walk back discussing his techniques. By the time you reach the Academy, you feel considerably better prepared for your next negotiation.

**Outcomes:**

- Level up **Charisma** attribute by 1.
- Ousmane friendship +1.

### Option B: Find a more profitable arrangement

While Ousmane is busy charming the merchant, you notice a stack of expensive imported components gathering dust behind the counter.

You interrupt the conversation with a suggestion: rather than merely negotiating a discount, why not arrange a trade? The Academy has plenty of aspiring artificers, and the merchant could use someone to help him find buyers for his more specialized merchandise.

The merchant considers this. Ousmane raises an appreciative eyebrow.

Between Ousmane's persuasive charm and your practical proposal, you soon negotiate a handsome commission for arranging several sales to Academy students.

Outside the shop, Ousmane counts out your share of the advance payment.

"Two hundred gold, Antonio! Not bad for an afternoon's work. I came here intending to spend money, and instead we're leaving richer."

He pauses thoughtfully.

"I should bring you shopping more often."

**Outcomes:**

- Gain **200 gold**.
- Ousmane respect +1.

### Option C: Forget the price. Look at the merchandise!

You have been listening to Ousmane's negotiations with growing fascination, but something else has caught your eye: a small collection of magical materials in a basket behind the counter.

You ask the merchant about them. He shrugs. They're leftover stock from an old shipment, too irregular to command a proper price.

Ousmane examines the basket and immediately understands your interest.

"My dear fellow! You have been sitting on a treasure without knowing it!"

The merchant looks suspicious. "A treasure?"

"To an artificer, certainly. To you, regrettably, a basket of things nobody wishes to buy. A tragedy for everyone involved."

After a few minutes of spirited discussion, Ousmane persuades the merchant to include some of the materials free of charge as a gesture of goodwill.

You leave with five perfectly usable moxes.

"Remember, Antonio," Ousmane says, patting you on the shoulder. "A good negotiator knows the price of everything. A great negotiator knows what things are worth."

**Outcomes:**

- Gain **5 moxes**.
- Ousmane friendship +1.
