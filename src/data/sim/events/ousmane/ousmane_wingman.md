# Event: Have You Met Antonio?

**Event ID:** `ousmane_wingman`

**Trigger:** Day ≥ 5, evening, Ousmane present, friendship with Ousmane ≥ 1.

**Invitation:** Ousmane invites you for a drink at the Old Monk's Inn. He promises an evening of good company and absolutely no trouble.

**Image:**: large

## Text

The Old Monk's Inn is lively tonight. Ousmane has secured a table with an excellent view of the common room, two mugs of ale, and, judging by his expression, a great many opinions about the people around you.

You're halfway through a perfectly pleasant conversation when you notice a pretty young woman sitting with a friend at a nearby table. She catches your eye and smiles. You return the smile, then quickly develop a fascinating interest in your drink.

Ousmane follows your gaze. A slow grin spreads beneath his moustache.

"Ah. I see."

You assure him there's nothing to see.

"Of course. My dear Antonio, I have spent years studying the behavior of men under pressure. I know the signs."

Before you can protest, he rises, straightens his jacket, and walks directly toward the young woman's table. He exchanges a few words with her, gestures in your direction, and somehow persuades her to follow him.

You have approximately four seconds to consider abandoning the Academy and beginning a new life as a shepherd.

"Have you met Antonio?" Ousmane announces cheerfully, presenting you as though unveiling a particularly fine piece of artificery. "An excellent fellow. Brilliant mind. Remarkable potential. Terrible at introducing himself."

The young woman laughs. "I'm Clara. Pleased to meet you."

"And now," Ousmane declares, "I shall leave you two to become acquainted."

He departs before either of you can object.

You watch him settle at another table, where he raises his mug in your direction with the solemn encouragement of a general sending his finest soldier into battle.

Clara sits down opposite you, still smiling.

"So, Antonio. Is your friend always like that?"

## Options

### Option A: Rise to the occasion

**Skill check:** Charisma — Medium

#### Success

You manage a reasonably witty response about Ousmane's unfortunate habit of recruiting people without their consent. Clara laughs, and the conversation finds its rhythm.

It turns out she's visiting friends in town and has an excellent sense of humor. You talk about the Academy, her travels, and the peculiarities of life among artificers. At some point, you realize you've entirely forgotten to be nervous.

Across the room, Ousmane is making an elaborate show of not watching you. When Clara rests her hand on yours, he nearly spills his drink.

The evening grows late. Clara suggests finding somewhere quieter, and the invitation in her eyes leaves very little room for misunderstanding.

You spare a final glance toward Ousmane. He gives you an enthusiastic thumbs-up, then pretends to study the ceiling.

You leave the inn together.

The precise details of the rest of your evening are, happily, none of Ousmane's business. Though you suspect this will not prevent him from expressing several opinions.

**Outcomes:**

- Charisma +1.
- Ousmane respect +1.
- Add story tag `ousmane_wingman_success`.

#### Failure

You begin confidently enough, but somewhere between describing your studies and attempting to explain the intricacies of Hordes, you lose your way.

Clara listens politely as you explain the strategic importance of positioning defensive units in the correct column. Why are you explaining this? You don't know. But you've started, and apparently there is no stopping.

"That's... very interesting," she says at last, with the careful expression of someone who has just been introduced to an unfamiliar medical condition.

A few minutes later, she excuses herself to rejoin her friend.

You stare mournfully into your drink.

Ousmane appears almost immediately and takes the chair opposite you.

"Well?"

You give him a look.

"Ah." He nods sympathetically. "A tactical defeat."

He orders another round.

"Listen, little brother. You went into battle. You survived. And next time, perhaps we shall try talking about something other than the deployment of imaginary infantry."

He claps you on the shoulder.

"Come. Drink. There are worse things in life than a pretty girl who doesn't appreciate a fine military formation."

**Outcomes:**

- Ousmane friendship +1.
- Add story tag `ousmane_wingman_failure`.

### Option B: Execute a strategic retreat

You give Clara an apologetic smile.

"Would you excuse me for just a moment?"

"Of course."

You stand, cross the room, and sit down opposite Ousmane.

He looks at you. Then at Clara. Then back at you.

"Antonio."

"Yes?"

"Did you just retreat from a woman who was already sitting at your table?"

You explain that you are merely regrouping.

"Regrouping." He considers this gravely. "An interesting strategy. I have seen men retreat from cavalry charges with less urgency."

You tell him that his intervention was entirely uncalled for.

"On the contrary! She was smiling. She was interested. I had accomplished ninety percent of the work!" He raises both hands in exasperation. "I cannot do everything for you, little brother. At some point, you must actually open your mouth."

He looks toward Clara, who is now chatting with her friend again.

"Ah well. Another time." He grins and pushes your mug toward you. "At least I have learned something valuable tonight."

You ask what.

"Never send an infantryman to do a cavalryman's job."

**Outcomes:**

- Ousmane friendship +1.
- Add story tag `ousmane_wingman_retreat`.

### Ousmane — The Art of Introduction

**Key:** `ousmane_memory_wingman`

**Type:** Memory

**Known by:** Ousmane

**When:** `ousmane_wingman_success`

Ousmane greets you with a broad grin. "Ah, Antonio! My finest pupil in the delicate art of romance." He leans closer. "I trust you remember who made the introduction? I require no payment. Although, should you ever name your firstborn after me, I would consider it a touching gesture."

**When:** `ousmane_wingman_failure`

Ousmane scratches his moustache thoughtfully. "I've been considering your performance at the Old Monk's Inn. Perhaps we should develop a new approach." You suggest that he leave you alone. "Impossible. I have invested too much in your education. We shall begin with a simple exercise: conversations that do not involve troop deployment."

**When:** `ousmane_wingman_retreat`

Ousmane looks at you with exaggerated concern. "Antonio, before we proceed, I must ask: am I standing too close? I wouldn't want you to execute another tactical retreat." You inform him that the joke is getting old. "Nonsense. A great military maneuver deserves to be remembered."

### Emma — A Little Bird Told Me

**Key:** `emma_memory_ousmane_wingman`

**Type:** Memory

**Known by:** Emma

**When:** `ousmane_wingman_success`

Emma looks unusually amused when you approach. "Antonio, I hear you've been conducting some extracurricular studies at the Old Monk's Inn." You ask who told her. "Ousmane, naturally. He appears to consider himself personally responsible for your entire social development." She smiles. "I'm pleased to hear you're making progress. Though I do hope you realize that not every successful encounter requires a commanding officer."

**When:** `ousmane_wingman_failure`

Emma gives you a curious look. "Ousmane tells me you attempted to seduce a young woman by explaining the tactical principles of Hordes." You protest that this is a gross exaggeration. "Oh? So you didn't explain the tactical principles of Hordes?" You hesitate. Emma's smile widens. "Antonio, I find your dedication to your studies admirable. But even I occasionally discuss other subjects."

**When:** `ousmane_wingman_retreat`

"Antonio," Emma begins pleasantly, "is it true that Ousmane introduced you to a pretty young woman, and you immediately ran away?" You explain that it was a tactical withdrawal. She presses her lips together, making a visible effort not to laugh. "I see. Well, should you ever wish to escape a conversation with me, do give me some warning. I'd hate to be trampled in the retreat."
