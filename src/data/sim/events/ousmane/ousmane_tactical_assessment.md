# Event: A Tactical Assessment

**Event ID:** `ousmane_tactical_assessment`  
**Trigger:** Day ≥ 3, evening.  
**Invitation:** Ousmane is arranging bread rolls and cutlery into what appears to be a military formation.
**Emotion**: Proud

## Text

You find Ousmane sitting at a cafeteria table, studying a Hordes board with the grave expression of a general contemplating a disastrous campaign. Beside it, several bread rolls have been arranged in neat rows, supported by an assortment of forks and spoons.

"Antonio! Excellent timing. I've been studying your tactics, and I believe I've identified your principal weakness."

You sit down, cautiously.

"You have no understanding of military discipline." He picks up a bread roll. "Observe. Infantry holds the center, cavalry protects the flanks, and reserves wait for the decisive moment."

He moves a piece of cheese behind the bread.

"Your approach, on the other hand, appears to consist of sending everyone forward and hoping the enemy dies of surprise."

Before you can defend your strategic genius, a passing student reaches over and takes one of the bread rolls.

Ousmane stares at the empty space.

"An enemy raid! Our left flank has collapsed!"

## Options

### Option A: Challenge his tactical theory

**Text:**

You point out that Hordes isn't exactly a conventional battlefield. A clever spell can overturn the strongest formation, and a single well-positioned unit can defeat a much larger force.

Ousmane considers this, then nods approvingly.

"A fair observation. Perhaps there is hope for you yet." He picks up the cheese. "Now, explain how you propose to counter my cavalry."

You spend the next quarter-hour arguing over tactics, occasionally moving the cheese and bread to illustrate your points. By the end, Ousmane is grinning.

"Good! A commander who argues with his superior. Dangerous, but promising."

**Outcomes:**

- Ousmane Respect +1.
- Intelligence check (medium): on success, +1 Erudition.

### Option B: Embrace the absurdity

**Text:**

You seize a spoon and plant it firmly behind Ousmane's lines.

"My lord, reinforcements have arrived! The Imperial Spoon Guard stands ready!"

Ousmane's face lights up.

"At last! A man of vision!"

Within minutes, the entire table has become a battlefield. Forks charge through the butter, bread rolls defend strategic positions, and a particularly heroic sausage launches a devastating offensive.

Unfortunately, your enthusiasm attracts the attention of the cafeteria staff.

"Are you gentlemen going to eat that food, or should I charge you rent for the battlefield?"

Ousmane surveys the devastation with dignity.

"A ceasefire, then. On humanitarian grounds."

**Outcomes:**

- Ousmane Friendship +1.
- Add memory `ousmane_memory_cafeteria_battle`, known by Ousmane: Ousmane grins when he sees you. "General Fibonacci! I trust the Imperial Spoon Guard remains in fighting condition. I've been reconsidering our defeat. With a second sausage, we might have held the center."

### Option C: Settle this on the battlefield

**Text:**

You push the bread aside and tap the Hordes board.

"Enough theory. If you're such a brilliant commander, prove it."

Ousmane leans back, his moustache lifting with a delighted grin.

"A challenge! From my own little brother! This is a proud day."

He produces his deck and begins shuffling.

"Let's make it interesting. Five mithril from my stores against a hundred gold from yours. The winner takes the prize. Unless, of course, your confidence has already deserted you?"

**Outcomes:**

- Start Hordes match against Ousmane.
- Ante: **Ousmane stakes 5 mithril; Antonio stakes 100 gold.**
- On victory: Antonio receives 5 mithril; Ousmane Respect +1.
- On defeat: Antonio loses 100 gold; Ousmane Friendship +1.
- Match only available if Antonio possesses at least 100 gold.
- Add memory `ousmane_memory_tactical_match`, known by Ousmane, with separate win/loss variants.

**Victory memory:** Ousmane folds his arms, looking at you with exaggerated suspicion. "I've reviewed our match, Antonio. Your tactics were sound, your timing excellent, and your victory entirely unacceptable. I demand a rematch. Preferably one that doesn't cost me mithril."

**Defeat memory:** Ousmane gives your shoulder a reassuring pat. "Never be discouraged by defeat, little brother. A hundred gold is a small price to pay for a valuable military education. Though I admit, it was a rather profitable lesson for the instructor."
