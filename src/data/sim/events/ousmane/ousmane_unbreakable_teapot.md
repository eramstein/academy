# Event: The Unbreakable Teapot

**Event ID:** `ousmane_unbreakable_teapot`  
**Trigger:** Day ≥ 5, evening  
**Invitation:** Ousmane is enthusiastically testing a new invention in the workshop.
**Place:** academy_workshop

## Text

You find Ousmane standing before a workbench, admiring a rather ordinary-looking teapot. His expression suggests that he has either achieved a major breakthrough in artificery or is about to do something spectacularly foolish.

"Antonio! Excellent timing, my young friend. Behold! The world's first truly indestructible teapot!"

He lifts it proudly. "Reinforced with black essence, a little mithril, and a technique of my grandfather's. It can withstand absolutely anything. Here, watch!"

Before you can object, he raises a heavy hammer and brings it down upon the teapot.

**CRASH!**

The teapot survives without a scratch. The workbench, unfortunately, does not. Its legs buckle, sending the teapot crashing through a rotten floorboard and into the storage room below.

A moment of silence follows.

Then comes the unmistakable sound of breaking crockery, followed by a very angry voice.

Ousmane looks at the hole, then at his hammer, then at you.

"Well," he declares, "I believe we can call the experiment a complete success."

You point out that the Academy may have a different interpretation of success.

"Nonsense! The teapot is intact. I never made any promises about the building."

He peers down through the hole. "Though perhaps we should retrieve it before someone asks questions."

A few minutes and a considerable amount of swearing later, you recover the teapot and clear away the wreckage. Ousmane surveys the damage with the air of a general assessing an unfortunate but ultimately victorious battle.

"My friend, you've been most helpful. Allow me to compensate you for your assistance. What would you prefer?"

- Add memory `ousmane_memory_unbreakable_teapot`, known by ousmane: Ousmane greets you with a broad grin. "Antonio! I've been considering improvements to our famous teapot." You suggest reinforcing the workbench this time. "An excellent thought! Though I was considering making the teapot even stronger." You remind him that the teapot wasn't the problem. "Details, my friend. You mustn't let details stand in the way of progress."

## Options

### Option A: "Teach me how you reinforced that thing."

**Text:**

Ousmane's face lights up.

"Ah! A man of science! I knew there was hope for you."

He launches into an enthusiastic explanation of black essence, structural reinforcement, and the remarkable properties of carefully layered enchantments.

His knowledge is impressive, and his teaching surprisingly clear. By the time he finishes, you've learned several techniques that would have taken you days to understand from a textbook.

"Remember, Antonio: black essence is about strength, stability, permanence. Properly applied, it can make almost anything indestructible."

You glance at the wrecked workbench.

"Yes, yes. I know what you're thinking. Next time, I'll reinforce the table too."

**Outcomes:**

- Teach color black: 10.

### Option B: "I'll take some of that mithril, if you're not using it."

**Text:**

Ousmane surveys the scattered remains of his experimental apparatus.

"An excellent choice! Practical, economical, and showing a healthy appreciation for valuable materials."

He rummages through the debris and produces several pieces of mithril, still perfectly usable despite the catastrophe.

"Here you are, my friend. Consider it your share of the spoils."

You remark that most people don't describe the destruction of their own furniture as a military victory.

"Most people lack imagination, Antonio."

**Outcomes:**

- Gain 5 mithril.

### Option C: "How about one of your cards?"

**Text:**

Ousmane smiles broadly.

"Ha! So the young artificer has developed an eye for quality. Very good!"

He retrieves a small leather case from a shelf, thankfully one that survived the experiment, and spreads several beautifully crafted cards before you.

"These are some of my own creations. Take your pick. A good card is worth far more than a teapot, even an indestructible one."

You examine the cards carefully while Ousmane watches with evident pride.

"Choose wisely, Antonio. And remember, if you ever make something that survives being hit with a hammer, do come and show me."

**Outcomes:**

- Get card: choose 1 of 3 cards from `ousmane,base`.
