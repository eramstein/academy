# Event: An Education in the Margins

**Event ID:** `molly_old_master_notes`  
**Trigger:** Day ≥ 5, afternoon
**Location:** Academy classroom  
**Type:** Optional event
**Invitation:** You notice an old book wedged beneath a classroom desk...

## Text

The afternoon lesson has barely begun when you discover that your desk has a slight wobble. A minor inconvenience, but one that threatens to send your inkwell on a journey across your trousers.

Investigating beneath the desk, you discover the culprit: a battered little book wedged between the woodwork and the floor. Apparently, some previous occupant considered literature an acceptable substitute for carpentry.

You pull it free. Its cover is faded, its pages yellowed, and nearly every margin is crammed with tiny handwriting. A name on the flyleaf identifies its former owner as a student who studied under one of the Academy's old masters, many decades ago.

You leaf through it with growing interest. The book contains wonderfully clear explanations of some notoriously difficult artificery principles, illustrated with clever diagrams and practical examples. Whoever wrote these notes had a gift for making complicated things seem almost embarrassingly simple.

Beside you, Molly is staring at her own textbook with the expression of someone contemplating an unpleasant medical procedure. She's been struggling with precisely this subject for days.

You could keep this little treasure for yourself. Or you could make someone's week considerably better.

## Options

### Option A: Give the book to Molly

**Text:**

You slide the book across the desk.

"Here. I think you'll find this useful."

Molly looks up, puzzled. She opens it, scans a page, then another. Her eyes widen.

"Oh! Wait, that's what the professor meant?" She flips back several pages. "But why didn't he just _say_ that?"

An excellent question, though perhaps not one to raise while the professor is standing within earshot.

Molly spends the next few minutes absorbed in the notes, occasionally making delighted little noises as one mystery after another resolves itself.

"Antonio, this is wonderful!" She beams at you. "Are you sure you don't want to keep it?"

You assure her that it's hers.

A little later, she rummages through her satchel and produces a playing card.

"I made this one myself. I was saving it, but..." She hesitates, then presses it into your hand. "I'd really like you to have it. It's only fair!"

**Outcomes:**

- Molly friendship +1.
- Increase story tag `molly_plot_academic_struggle` by 2.
- Give Antonio one random card from the `base,molly` collection, selected from three possibilities.
- Add memory `molly_memory_old_master_notes`, known by Molly: "Molly smiles when the subject of studying comes up. 'I've been using that old notebook you found. It's amazing! I actually understood half of yesterday's lecture.' She pauses. 'The other half remains a mystery, but I'm choosing to celebrate the progress.'"

### Option B: Keep the book

**Text:**

You slip the little volume into your satchel. Whoever wrote these notes clearly understood artificery, and possessing a personal guide to the Academy's more obscure principles could prove invaluable.

Molly leans over, curious.

"What's that?"

"An old student's notebook. Quite a good one, actually."

"Oh, lucky you!" she says cheerfully, before returning to her textbook with a sigh.

You glance at the book in your satchel. It really is a remarkable find. The diagrams alone might save you hours of work.

**Outcomes:**

- Give player artifact `artificery_for_dummies`.
