# Event: A Little Autumn Madness

**Event ID:** `molly_autumn_leaves`  
**Invitation:** Molly looks rather miserable, sitting alone with her books.  
**Trigger:** Afternoon, starting from day 10.  
**Location:** Library  
**Type:** Optional event

## Text

You find Molly sitting alone at a table, surrounded by a formidable collection of textbooks and crumpled notes. Her usual cheerful expression is nowhere to be seen. She has her head in her hands, staring at a page as though it has personally insulted her.

"Oh, hello, Antonio." She manages a weak smile. "I've been studying all morning, and I think I know less than when I started. Every time I understand one thing, three more things stop making sense."

She looks down at her notes. "What if I'm just not clever enough for this place?"

You could offer to help her study, but somehow you suspect another hour of artificery theory is the very last thing she needs. Outside the tall windows, the courtyard trees are shedding their golden leaves in splendid quantities.

An idea occurs to you.

"Come on, Molly. We're going outside."

"Outside? But I have to—"

"Doctor's orders. Very serious condition. Acute textbook poisoning."

That earns you the beginnings of a smile.

## Options

### Option A: Take her outside for some fresh air

**Text:**

Molly reluctantly lets you lead her into the courtyard. The afternoon air is crisp, the sky pale blue, and the ground is covered in a thick carpet of yellow and copper leaves.

You walk together for a while, talking about nothing important. Gradually, Molly's shoulders relax, and her familiar smile returns.

Then you spot a particularly magnificent pile of leaves beside an old oak tree.

An irresistible opportunity.

You scoop up a generous handful and toss them at her.

Molly stares at you in astonishment as the leaves flutter down around her.

"Antonio Fibonacci!"

For one terrible moment, you wonder whether you've misjudged the situation. Then she bends down, gathers an enormous armful, and launches a counterattack with the enthusiasm of a seasoned artillery commander.

The battle escalates rapidly. Molly has the advantage in strength, speed, and an alarming willingness to throw herself bodily into the larger piles. You are eventually overwhelmed, and the two of you collapse into the leaves, breathless with laughter.

For a while, you simply lie there, looking up through the thinning branches.

"Thank you," Molly says quietly. "I really needed this."

She turns her head toward you, smiling. "You know, sometimes I forget we're allowed to enjoy ourselves."

After a moment, she sits up and rummages through her satchel.

"Oh! I nearly forgot. I have a few cards I made recently. Pick whichever one you like!"

"What's the occasion?"

"Consider it a prize for services rendered to a very distressed student." She grins. "And for being such a wonderfully terrible leaf fighter."
