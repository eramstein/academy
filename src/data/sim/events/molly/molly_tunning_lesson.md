Event: The Running Lesson
NPC: molly
Event ID: molly_running_lesson
Trigger: day >= 4; afternoon
Image: large

Text
The Academy apparently believes that a sound mind requires sound legs. Today's afternoon lesson therefore takes place in the courtyard, where a physical-training instructor has ordered the students to run laps around the grounds. You join the procession, trying to decide whether this counts as education or an elaborate punishment.

A few strides ahead, Molly is in magnificent spirits. She runs with an easy, springy gait, chatting to Emma as though the two of them were enjoying a leisurely walk through a garden.

"And then I told the stable boy that of course he could bring the foal indoors, just until the rain stopped—oh, Antonio! Hello! Isn't this lovely?"

She waves enthusiastically and, without noticing, picks up the pace. Emma follows with considerably less enthusiasm. Her face is composed, but her breathing suggests that composing it is taking a heroic effort.

"Lovely," Emma manages. "An inspired word for... running in circles."

Molly laughs. "Oh, you'll feel wonderful afterward! It's just a matter of finding your rhythm."

Emma looks at you. It is a remarkable look: simultaneously a request for mercy and a warning not to enjoy this too much.

Options
Option A: Challenge Molly to a race
Check: vitality hard

On success:

Text:

"Race you to the gate, Molly!"

Her eyes light up. "Really? Oh, splendid!"

She surges forward. You follow, somehow finding another reserve of speed, and the two of you thunder past the fountain almost shoulder to shoulder. At the gate you make one final, desperate effort and reach the stone post a fraction ahead of her.

Molly stops, breathless and delighted. "Antonio! That was wonderful! I didn't know you could run like that!" She gives your shoulder an approving squeeze. "We absolutely must train together. I run on Monday evenings, here in the courtyard. You should come!"

Behind you, Emma arrives at a more dignified pace. "Congratulations," she says between breaths. "You've both successfully reached a gate. Truly, a historic day for scholarship."

  Option B: Give Molly a gentle hint
  Text:

You fall into step beside Molly and lower your voice. "I think Emma might appreciate a slightly more leisurely pace."

Molly glances over her shoulder. Emma is several strides back, pursuing the conversation with the grim determination of someone pursuing an escaping carriage.

"Oh!" Molly slows at once. "Emma, I'm so sorry! I didn't even notice!"

"No apology necessary," Emma says, drawing alongside you. "I was merely considering whether the Academy offers correspondence courses in physical education."

Molly giggles, then turns to you with an appreciative smile. "Thank you for telling me, Antonio. I do get carried away. You know, I run here on Monday evenings. Would you like to join me sometime? We could go at whatever pace suits you."

Emma gives you the faintest nod of gratitude. It's the sort of gesture that might be missed by anyone who hadn't just saved her from another lap at Molly's preferred speed.
