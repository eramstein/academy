Text
You encounter Molly in the Academy courtyard just as she's finishing an afternoon of exercise. Her hair has escaped its ribbon, her cheeks are pink, and she looks entirely too pleased with herself for someone who has apparently spent the last hour being thrown about on a patch of grass.

"Antonio!" She hurries over and takes your hand in greeting. Her grip is firm. Very firm. For one alarming instant, you wonder whether the Academy has secretly installed a mechanical hand beneath her sleeve.

"Good heavens, Molly. Do you crack walnuts with that thing?"

"Sometimes!" She laughs. "Well, not often. It's rather messy."

She notices you flexing your fingers and looks suddenly apologetic. "Oh! Did I squeeze too hard? I'm sorry. I've been practicing wrestling since I was little. My father's guards taught me. They said I was too small to manage the larger boys, so naturally I had to prove them wrong."

Her embarrassment gives way to a mischievous smile. "Actually, I think I could take you."

You look at this cheerful young woman, considerably smaller than you, with her loose hair and innocent expression. One doesn't often receive such a generous invitation to make a fool of oneself.

"Would you like to try?" she asks eagerly. "Just a friendly bout! First to pin the other's shoulders, and no rough stuff."

You agree, and Molly immediately shows you where to stand. She's surprisingly serious about the rules: no striking, no dangerous holds, and the moment either of you taps out, the match ends. Then she offers a bright smile. "Ready?"

You are barely ready before she comes at you. Her first move is so quick that you stumble, and only a desperate shift of balance keeps you upright. Molly laughs, delighted, and tries to take your leg. This time you anticipate her, turn with the movement, and manage to bring her down onto the grass.

For a moment you find yourselves in an absurd tangle of limbs. Molly attempts to escape with a strength that seems altogether unreasonable for her size, but you manage to pin both shoulders before she can roll free.

"Oh!" She blinks up at you, then bursts out laughing. "You got me! Properly got me!"

You release her and help her up. She accepts your hand with a grin, brushes grass from her clothes, and shakes yours solemnly—as though the two of you have just concluded an important international treaty.

"I owe you a prize!" She rummages through the small satchel beside the practice ground and produces a card. "There. A victory deserves something. And don't think this means I won't ask for a rematch."
