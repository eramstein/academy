Event ID: molly_first_encounter
Trigger: current acitivty type is Class, period is afternoon

Text

Your first artificery lesson is about to begin, and the classroom is filling with students, books, and the quiet anxiety of people who have just realized they are expected to know things. You find an empty seat beside a young woman who is carefully arranging her notes into a very neat pile. She looks up and offers you a shy but welcoming smile.

"Hello! I'm Molly. Molly Moreno. Are you new too?" She seems relieved when you nod. "Oh, good. Not that being new is good, exactly. It's just nice not to be the only one who has no idea what we're doing."

She glances at the complicated magical diagrams covering the blackboard, then at her notes. "I'm sure it'll make sense eventually. Everyone says the first year is difficult." A pause. "Though I suspect they mean it gets worse."

The professor enters, and Molly hurriedly pushes a spare sheet of parchment toward you. "Here, you can use this. And if you need to borrow anything, just ask."

She smiles again, more confidently this time. At least one person in the Academy seems pleased to have you here.
