# Event: An Invitation from Molly

**NPC:** molly  
**Event ID:** `molly_pajama_party`  
**Trigger:** day >= 10; Molly friendship >= 2;
**Image:** large  
**Emotion:** happy

## Text

The cafeteria is busy when Molly spots you coming through the door. She waves so vigorously that two students turn around to see whom she's greeting.

"Antonio! Just the person I wanted!" She hurries over, glowing with excitement. "Would you like to spend the night with me?"

A spoon clatters onto a plate at the next table.

Well. That is unusually direct.

_She means an evening together,_ you tell yourself. _Perhaps a late supper._

Of course. A perfectly sensible interpretation. And yet she did say _the night_.

Molly doesn't notice your sudden interest in precise vocabulary. "I've been planning it for days! I've already bought the chocolate, and there are plenty of pillows. You won't need to bring a thing."

The conversation around you seems to have developed an unexpected interest in silence. A young man behind Molly raises his eyebrows so high they threaten to disappear into his hair.

_Chocolate. Pillows. Her room._

Steady, Fibonacci. You have known this girl for approximately five minutes in the grand scheme of human relationships. There must be some innocent explanation.

"At the Royal Pigeon," she adds helpfully. "My room's quite large. And don't worry, I have something you can wear!"

An innocent explanation is becoming distressingly difficult to construct.

Around you, several students are now listening with shameless attention. Molly glances at them, then back at you. "So? Would you like to come?"

You are rapidly acquiring a reputation without having made a single decision. Which, admittedly, is an efficient way to acquire one.

## Node: the_party

**Title:** Pillows and Chocolate  
**Emotion:** happy

The Royal Pigeon Inn has never looked more respectable. Its polished corridors, velvet carpets, and discreetly attentive staff seem designed to assure wealthy travelers that nothing undignified has ever happened within its walls.

On the way upstairs, you remind yourself to be mature, considerate, and above all prepared for whatever Molly has in mind. _Naturally._ The fact that you have adjusted your collar three times is merely a coincidence.

Molly opens the door with a brilliant smile. "You're here! Come in, come in!"

Her room makes a brave attempt to challenge the inn's reputation.

The chairs have been pushed aside, cushions cover the carpet, and an impressive mountain of pillows occupies the middle of the room. A tray holds two steaming mugs of chocolate and enough biscuits to sustain a small military campaign. Molly is wearing a comfortable cream linen nightshirt and loose trousers, her short hair even more unruly than usual.

_Pillows everywhere. A nightshirt. A private room._

Your imagination rallies for one last, rather heroic effort.

"I made us a fort!" Molly announces proudly, pointing at an arrangement of cushions and blankets. "And I found my old favorite pajamas for you. I hope these fit. They're rather large on me, but you are rather large compared with me, so it should work out splendidly."

Oh.

_Oh._

The truth arrives with the slow, terrible majesty of a collapsing cathedral. She means a pajama party. An actual pajama party. With pajamas.

For one brief, shameful moment, you contemplate the distance between the evening you imagined and the evening arranged before you. Then Molly holds up the spare trousers, looking so delighted by her preparations that the disappointment begins to seem rather foolish.

The trousers fit. The top is a little short in the sleeves. Molly considers you for a moment and nods with satisfaction.

"Perfect! Now, the first rule of pajama parties is that nobody has to be terribly grown-up. The second rule is that we mustn't spill chocolate on Margaret's carpet. She was _very_ particular about that."

"And the third rule?" you ask.

"Oh, there isn't one. I only thought of two." She takes a mug, settles cross-legged among the cushions, and looks up at you expectantly.

You take a biscuit. The chocolate smells wonderful. Against all expectations—and perhaps against your better judgment—you find yourself smiling.

### Option A: Declare a pillow war

**Text:**

You pick up a pillow and examine it thoughtfully. "And what, exactly, are the rules concerning armed conflict?"

Molly's eyes widen. "Oh, no, you don't—"

Your first strike catches her harmlessly on the shoulder. She retaliates immediately, and with such enthusiasm that you are forced into a strategic retreat behind an armchair. For several glorious minutes, the Royal Pigeon becomes the scene of a battle that military historians will never be permitted to study.

At last Molly collapses onto the cushions, breathless with laughter. "Truce! Truce! I surrender!"

There is a soft knock at the door.

"Miss Moreno?" comes Margaret's impeccably controlled voice. "Is everything quite all right?"

You and Molly look at each other. A feather floats slowly between you.

"Perfectly!" Molly calls. "We're being very quiet!"

The silence on the other side of the door is eloquent. Then Margaret's footsteps retreat down the corridor.

Molly presses a pillow over her mouth to smother another fit of giggles. A moment later you're laughing too, with no trace left of the elaborate evening your imagination had prepared. This is, rather inconveniently, much more fun.

### Option B: Settle in for chocolate and stories

**Text:**

You accept a mug and settle beside Molly among the cushions. "I think I'd rather begin with the chocolate. You promised something magnificent."

"And you shall have it!" She offers you the biscuit tin with great ceremony. "My grandmother used to make hot chocolate for all the cousins during winter visits. We would stay up telling stories until someone fell asleep in the middle of one. Usually me."

She recounts a childhood expedition to rescue a kitten from an apple tree, which apparently ended with three children, one confused gardener, and a ladder stuck in a pond. You contribute a story of your own. Molly listens with the rapt attention of someone for whom another person's adventures are as exciting as her own.

After a while she draws her knees up beneath her nightshirt and smiles into her mug. "It's nice, isn't it? Just being silly and comfortable with someone. No lessons, no examinations, nobody expecting us to accomplish anything important."

For once, neither of you feels obliged to improve the evening by doing anything at all. Whatever you thought you were coming here for, you discover that a mug of excellent chocolate and Molly's delighted company are very difficult to improve upon.
