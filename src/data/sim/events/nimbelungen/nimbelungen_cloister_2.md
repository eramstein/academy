Text
Well, Professor Nimbelungen wasn't lying.

Finding the hidden cloister was easy enough, once you knew where to look. Getting inside was another matter entirely.

The stone statue guarding the entrance had come to life the moment you approached, stepped down from its pedestal, and challenged you to a game of Hordes.

Not a fight to the death. Not a riddle. A perfectly ordinary game of Hordes.

Well, ordinary apart from the fact that your opponent was made of granite.

After a surprisingly difficult match, the statue had grudgingly acknowledged your victory, stepped aside, and returned to its pedestal.

Apparently, Professor Nimbelungen enjoys turning ancient architectural features into card-playing doormen.

You wonder whether the Academy administration is aware of this particular hobby.

Probably best not to ask.

Beyond the entrance, you find yourself in a small, forgotten cloister. Ivy climbs the weathered columns, weeds push through the flagstones, and a dry fountain sits in the middle of a peaceful courtyard.

It's rather beautiful, actually.

You explore the place, searching for the treasure Nimbelungen promised.

Under a loose stone near the fountain, you discover a small folded piece of parchment.

At last!

You unfold it eagerly.

Three words are written in a hurried, rather untidy hand.

Follow the rabbit.

You turn the parchment over.

Nothing.

You hold it up to the light.

Still nothing.

"Follow the rabbit?"

You look around. No rabbits. No obvious rabbit-shaped statues. Not even a particularly suspicious-looking carrot.

You sigh.

Of course. You should have known Professor Nimbelungen's treasure hunt wouldn't end with something as vulgar as actual treasure.

Still, you've come this far.

You carefully fold the note and slip it into your pocket.

Somewhere in this Academy, there's apparently a rabbit worth following.

And knowing Nimbelungen, it probably plays Hordes too.
