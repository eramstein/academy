NPC: nimbelungen
Trigger: Monday evening; leagues won = 1

Text
It's Monday evening, and the Academy corridors are finally growing quiet.

You're making your way back from a long day of lessons when a voice calls out behind you.

"Fibonacci! Antonio Fibonacci!"

You turn to find a professor approaching at considerable speed, his long, oily brown hair swinging around his shoulders. His robes are slightly disheveled, and he carries a deck of Hordes cards in one hand and what appears to be a brass compass in the other.

He stops abruptly in front of you.

"Splendid! Just splendid! Congratulations!"

"Thank you, Professor...?"

"Nimbelungen! Professor Nimbelungen. Of course you wouldn't know me. Haven't taught your class yet. Might never. Timetables are dreadfully restrictive things."

He waves the compass dismissively.

"Your league victory! Excellent performance. Very promising. Though I thought your opening could have been improved. A little too cautious. But the third turn! Ha! Very clever."

You open your mouth to respond, but he has already moved on.

"Tell me, Fibonacci, do you enjoy treasure hunts?"

"Treasure hunts?"

"Yes, yes! Clues! Secrets! Hidden passages! The occasional dangerous guardian! Wonderful exercise for the mind."

He leans closer, lowering his voice to a conspiratorial whisper.

"You see, the Academy is a very old place. Older than most of its professors, which is saying something. And it has secrets. Rooms nobody remembers. Passages that don't appear on any map. Places the administration would very much prefer everyone forgot."

He straightens, looking delighted.

"So naturally, I've made a hobby of hiding treasures in them!"

You blink.

"You're hiding treasures around the Academy?"

"Precisely! For the most promising students. A proper education requires more than sitting in classrooms and listening to old men talk. One must explore! Deduce! Take risks!"

He pauses thoughtfully.

"And play Hordes, of course. Everything worth having should require at least one good match."

"Of course."

"Now, there's a particularly interesting place I think you might appreciate. The hidden cloister."

"The Academy has a hidden cloister?"

"Certainly! Most respectable academies do. Ours happens to be rather well hidden."

He gestures vaguely toward the courtyard.

"Go to the Academy courtyard. Along the eastern wall, behind the old ivy-covered fountain. There's a narrow archway, almost completely concealed by the vines. Follow the passage beyond it, and you'll find the cloister."

He taps the side of his nose.

"But don't expect to simply stroll in and collect your reward! I've arranged a little challenge. A guardian, you see. Quite an agreeable fellow, once you've beaten him."

"A guardian?"

"Well, technically a statue. But that rather undersells him."

You stare at the professor.

He stares back, beaming.

Then, without warning, he looks down at the deck in his hand.

"Ah! That reminds me. If I replace these two cards with a cheaper unit, the entire opening becomes—oh, that's beautiful!"

He begins rearranging cards at a furious pace.

"Professor? About the cloister—"

"Yes, yes! Eastern wall! Fountain! Guardian! Good luck, Fibonacci!"

And with that, he hurries down the corridor, muttering something about mana curves and the mathematical elegance of a perfectly timed counterattack.

You watch him disappear around a corner.

Well.

That was certainly an encounter.

You're not entirely sure whether Professor Nimbelungen is a brilliant scholar, a dangerous lunatic, or simply a man who has played far too much Hordes.

Possibly all three.

Still, the hidden cloister sounds intriguing. And if there's one thing you've learned about this Academy, it's that the strangest rumors have an irritating tendency to turn out to be true.

You make a mental note to investigate the courtyard one of these days.

Preferably before the professor decides to hide something even more dangerous.
