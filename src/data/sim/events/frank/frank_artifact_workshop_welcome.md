# Event: A Few Patterns to Get You Started

**Event ID:** `frank_artifact_workshop_welcome`  
**Trigger:** First visit to `academy_workshop` after `frank_artifact_workshop_intro`. One-time event.  
**Image:** large  
**Emotion:** happy

## Text

The Academy workshop smells of hot metal, sawdust, and something faintly sweet that is probably best left unidentified. Tools hang in neat rows above crowded benches. Along one wall, a collection of unfinished contraptions clicks, hums, and occasionally shudders.

Frank stands at an anvil, holding a small piece of metal in a pair of tongs. He spots you, breaks into a grin, and sets it aside.

"There you are! Come in, come in. Mind the bucket."

You step around a bucket full of bent brass rods.

"Other bucket."

You narrowly avoid a second one.

"Excellent. Good awareness of your surroundings. Essential workshop skill."

He wipes his hands on his apron and clears a space on the nearest bench, shifting an alarming quantity of metal with one enormous forearm. From a drawer, he produces four rolled sheets of thick paper.

"Now, I promised you a few patterns. **Three tools and a trinket.** Simple enough to start with, useful enough to keep."

He spreads out the first sheet and holds its curling corners down with a hammer and a mug.

"**Basic Stabilisator.** Keeps the matter you're working with steady while you shape it. Helps with precision, gives you a little more mastery over the work. Black magic, a bit of silver, and a steady hand. Preferably yours."

The next sheet is covered in neatly ruled diagrams and cramped annotations.

"**Basic Codex.** Helps you keep track of the crafting process. What you've done, what comes next, which step you were absolutely certain you could skip. Good for your erudition. You'll need blue magic and some mox for that one."

He unrolls the third pattern, revealing a curious arrangement of small apertures.

"**Basic Porticuli.** Gives you glimpses of what might be possible. Handy when you're short on inspiration. Red magic and magic dust. Don't spend all afternoon peering into it, though. At some point you've got to actually make something."

The final sheet bears the drawing of an amulet.

"And a **Vitality Amulet**. A little something to put a bit more life in you. Green magic, magic dust. Useful outside the workshop, too. Especially if your idea of lunch is whatever you can eat while running to your next lesson."

He gathers the four patterns and pushes them towards you.

"These are yours. No charge for the patterns. **Materials are still your responsibility**, mind. Each one takes a hundred gold's worth of supplies, plus five of the material marked on the sheet. You'll need a proper grasp of its color, too."

You look over the diagrams. They seem considerably less simple now that they are in front of you.

Frank notices your expression.

"Don't worry about making all four at once. Pick something you can manage and take it one step at a time. Read the pattern before you start, measure before you cut, and ask if you're unsure."

Something behind him gives a sharp metallic ping.

Without looking round, Frank reaches back and catches a small brass wheel.

"And keep an eye on your surroundings."

He drops the wheel into the first bucket and gives you a cheerful nod.

"Right, then. Let's see what you can make!"
