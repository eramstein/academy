Emma Act 2 — The Fairy, the Pirate, and the Italian Codex

Trigger: from Day 22, morning
Image: large

Text

Professor Lombard arrives at the lectern carrying a stack of scripts, a rolled-up poster, and the expression of a woman about to make her problem everyone else's.

"Students! In the interest of culture, cooperation, and demonstrating that this Academy produces more than people who can set a laboratory on fire, we shall stage a theatrical production of Peter Pan."

A silence falls. Even the apparatus at the back of the room seems to stop rattling.

"Participation is mandatory. Rehearsal will take place next Sunday afternoon at the Academy theater. I have assigned the roles myself."

She consults her list.

"Antonio Fibonacci: Peter Pan. Emma Delamain: Tinker Bell."

Emma slowly looks up from her notes. "A fairy?"

"A very important fairy," Lombard replies.

"One who is approximately six inches tall and communicates by jingling?"

"I'm confident you'll rise to the challenge, Miss Delamain."

"I was rather hoping to rise above six inches."

Molly's hand shoots into the air. "Professor! Could I be the crocodile? Please? I can make the noise!"

Before anyone can stop her, she demonstrates a terrifyingly enthusiastic ticking sound, complete with snapping jaws. Several students instinctively pull their feet under their chairs.

"The crocodile is yours, Miss Molly," Lombard says, hurriedly.

Farid examines the cast list over his neighbor's shoulder. "Captain Hook. Of course I'm the pirate guy. One time I bring a knife to class, and suddenly it's my whole personality."

"You brought three," Emma observes.

"They were different knives."

Professor Lombard claps sharply. "Wonderful! Such enthusiasm. Costumes will be provided. Please learn your lines before Sunday."

Emma leans toward you as the lesson resumes.

"Well, Peter. If you expect me to save you from pirates, I shall require considerably better working conditions than a thimble for a bedroom."

"I'll take it up with management."

"Do. And ask them whether the fairy is allowed to speak."
