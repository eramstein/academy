Event: Happy Little Accident

Event ID: happy_little_accident

Trigger: day 4, morning

Text

Your third artificery lesson is progressing remarkably well. You've arranged your ingredients, prepared your pigments, and even managed to follow most of the professor's instructions. At this rate, you might produce something almost respectable.

Then your elbow catches a jar.

A cascade of shimmering blue powder spills across the workbench, mixing with your carefully prepared ingredients. You grab for a cloth, knock over a second jar, and watch in horror as the entire mixture begins to glow.

There is a sharp pop, a flash of blue light, and the unmistakable smell of burnt cinnamon.

When the smoke clears, a perfectly formed card lies in the middle of the table.

You pick it up cautiously. The enchantments woven into its surface are unlike anything you've seen before: intricate, powerful, and somehow perfectly balanced.

The professor approaches, examines your creation, and falls silent.

"Fibonacci. How exactly did you achieve this?"

An excellent question. One you would very much like to know the answer to yourself.

"Remarkable," the professor murmurs. "Quite remarkable. You must document your procedure."

Yes. Of course. Your procedure.

You glance at the empty jars, the stained workbench, and the smoking remains of your notes.

This could be difficult.
