# Event: A Rather Unfortunate First Impression

**NPC:** emma.

**Event ID:** `emma_first_encounter`

**Trigger:** First time Emma is present at the player's location.

**Image:** large.

**Emotion:** neutral.

## Text

Well, that's done. First year's tuition paid.

And you're broke again.

Three years of savings, gone in the time it took an administrator to stamp a piece of paper. You'd better pass those exams. Preferably with honors. Preferably without starving in the meantime.

You make your way through the crowded Academy corridor, dodging students, luggage, and an elderly professor who appears to be arguing with his own hat.

That's when you notice her.

A young woman is studying the timetable pinned to the noticeboard. First year, judging by the schedule she's examining. Like you, then.

Unlike you, she seems to know exactly what she's doing.

She turns toward the village, carrying a precarious tower of books that looks as though it might require its own structural engineer.

Naturally, you decide to offer your assistance.

A perfectly chivalrous impulse. One that has absolutely nothing to do with the long, graceful legs visible beneath her skirt.

You catch up and call out to her.

She turns.

Bright green eyes settle on you, appraising you with an intelligence that would probably be intimidating if you were currently capable of rational thought.

You should introduce yourself.

Yes. That would be sensible.

Or perhaps explain why you've stopped her.

Also sensible.

You could even try speaking. People do that, apparently.

"Take your time," she says at last, one eyebrow rising. "It's not as though I'm carrying half the Academy library."

Her expression hovers somewhere between amusement and resignation.

You have the distinct impression that this is not the first time she's had this effect on a young man.

## Effects

- Add to Emma's known traits:
  - **First impressions:** A striking young woman with sharp green eyes, an impressive collection of books, and an equally impressive talent for making you feel foolish. Apparently a fellow first-year student.

## Options

### Option A: Pull yourself together

**Text:**

You clear your throat, summon the scattered remains of your dignity, and manage a smile.

"Sorry. I was going to ask if you needed a hand. Antonio, by the way."

"Emma. A pleasure, Antonio."

She adjusts the stack of books with surprising ease.

"And thank you, but I'm rather stronger than I look."

With a quick wink, she strides away toward the village, leaving you with the uncomfortable suspicion that she has already formed a fairly accurate opinion of you.

**Outcomes:**

- No additional game-state effects.

### Option B: A complete failure of higher reasoning

**Text:**

This is absurd.

You've barely arrived at the Academy, and already you're being subjected to this sort of sorcery. Without warning. On the very first day.

Surely there ought to be regulations.

Nobody should be allowed to have legs like that without some sort of advance notice.

"Emma, by the way," she says, rescuing the conversation from its untimely death. "Delighted to meet you, mysterious stranger."

She sets off at a brisk pace.

A few steps later, she glances back over her shoulder.

"Oh, and one more thing..."

A mischievous smile curls at the corner of her mouth.

"The dark, silent, mysterious type? Doesn't work on me."

And with that, she disappears into the crowd.

You remain where you are for several seconds, contemplating what may well be the most spectacular conversational defeat of your academic career.
