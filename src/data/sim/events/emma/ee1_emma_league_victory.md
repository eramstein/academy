# Event: League Champion

**Event ID:** `emma_league_victory`

**Trigger:** Antonio wins a league season. Activity is LeagueCeremony.

## Text

The applause has barely died down when Emma approaches you, a glass of sparkling wine in hand.

"Well, well. Antonio Fibonacci, league champion. I suppose I'll have to start taking you seriously."

Her teasing smile gives way to something more sincere.

"Congratulations, Antonio. Truly. You've earned this. Winning a match is one thing, but maintaining that level of play throughout an entire season? That's quite an achievement."

She raises her glass in your honor.

## Effects

- Emma respect +3.

## Options

### Option A: Accept graciously

**Text:**

"Thank you, Emma. That means a lot, coming from you."

Her expression softens into a warm smile.

"Don't get too sentimental, Fibonacci. I'm still planning to take that title from you next season."

She touches her glass to yours.

"But tonight, it's yours. Enjoy it."

**Outcomes:**

- Emma friendship +1.

### Option B: A little friendly provocation

**Text:**

"Thank you, Emma. And don't worry, I'll try to leave a few victories for the rest of you next season."

Emma raises an eyebrow.

"How magnanimous. Shall I inform the Academy that its new champion has generously agreed to let us participate?"

"Only seems fair."

She laughs, shaking her head.

"Enjoy your reign, Fibonacci. I'll be the first to celebrate when it ends."

"Celebrate?"

"Extravagantly. I may even commission a commemorative plaque."

She raises her glass again, her eyes sparkling with amusement.

**Outcomes:**

- Emma rivalry +1.
- Emma friendship +1.
