Event: A Worthy Opponent

Event ID: emma_first_match_victory

Theme: Competition

Trigger: Antonio wins his first competitive match against Emma.

Image: large

Text

This is a risky move. Perhaps a little too aggressive? Well, fortune favors the bold. According to Henry, Emma Delamain is probably the strongest player at the Academy. After you, naturally.

You commit to the attack.

She doesn't see it coming. Victory!

Across the table, Emma realizes her mistake immediately—but a quarter of a second too late. A flash of anger crosses her delicate, heart-shaped face. Her shoulders sink, and for a brief moment she looks almost wounded. Then she straightens, gathers her composure, and meets your eyes.

"Well played, Fibonacci."

She glances at the board once more, her brow furrowing. For a moment, she seems to be debating something with herself.

"Tell me. Was that a bluff, or had you calculated it from the beginning?"

Options

Option A: Explain your strategy

Text:

You conceal your considerable satisfaction and begin explaining your reasoning, starting several turns before the decisive attack. Emma listens without interrupting, her eyes moving between you and the cards still scattered across the table.

Then comes a question. A very precise question. You answer it, only to be met with another. Before long, the two of you are reconstructing the entire match, debating alternatives, and examining moves neither of you had considered at the time.

Emma's bitterness gradually disappears, replaced by intense concentration. At one point she even smiles, apparently delighted to have discovered a particularly elegant possibility. Losing, it seems, becomes considerably more tolerable when there is something interesting to learn from it.

Eventually, she rises, a little stiffly, and gathers her belongings. She regards you for a long moment, her expression thoughtful.

"Interesting, Antonio. Very interesting." A faint smile touches her lips. "Until next time."

Option B: Enjoy your victory

Text:

Oh, this is too good. The Academy's brightest star, defeated by Antonio Fibonacci himself! Surely a little celebration is in order.

"Bluff? Me?" You give an extravagant shrug. "My dear Delamain, I was merely trying to give you a sporting chance."

Emma's eyes narrow. For a moment, you wonder whether you've pushed your luck.

"A sporting chance?" she repeats, very slowly.

"Well, I wouldn't want you to lose confidence. You have such a promising future."

Emma stares at you. Then, despite herself, a reluctant smile breaks through.

"How extraordinarily generous of you, Fibonacci. I shall treasure your encouragement."

She begins gathering her cards, then pauses.

"Although I do hope you're prepared for our next match."

"Naturally."

"Good. Because I intend to make you regret every single word of this conversation."

She flashes you a dazzling smile.

"And I shall be insufferably gracious about it."

Outcomes:

Emma: rivalry +1

Option C: Attempt a charming reply

Condition: Antonio has the emma_smitten tag.

Text:

Oh, no. She's waiting for an answer.

This is your chance, Antonio. You've just defeated the most formidable player at the Academy. Surely you can manage a few clever words. Something confident. Something memorable. Something that will make her look at you differently.

You open your mouth.

And then she looks at you with those eyes.

Really, this is most unfair. How is a man supposed to think under such circumstances? You had an entire strategy worked out a moment ago. Where has it gone? Perhaps it's hiding under the table.

"Well, I... that is, the thing about the attack..."

Emma raises an eyebrow. Then recognition dawns, and the corner of her mouth curls upward.

"Ah, yes. My silent admirer. I remember you."

She allows herself a small, thoroughly delighted smile.

"Well, it seems you're rather better at strategy than rhetoric."

She gathers her cards and stands. Her expression softens for a fleeting moment, the teasing smile becoming something almost affectionate.

"Until next time, Antonio."

And with that, she walks away, leaving you with a magnificent victory, a thoroughly ruined speech, and the uncomfortable suspicion that she enjoyed every second of it.

Outcomes:

Emma: love +1
