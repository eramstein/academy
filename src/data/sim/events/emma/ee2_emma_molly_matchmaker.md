# Event: A Rather Promising Match

**NPC:** emma  
**Event ID:** `emma_molly_matchmaker`  
**Trigger:** Evening; story tag `emma_trusted_companion`; Emma friendship ≥ 3  
**Image:** medium

## Text

It's a pleasant evening at the inn. You and Emma have settled into a quiet corner for dinner, enjoying the rare luxury of having absolutely nothing important to discuss.

Molly has just left after spending a cheerful half-hour with you both. Emma watches her disappear through the door, a thoughtful smile on her lips.

"You know, Antonio, I've been meaning to ask you something."

She sets down her glass.

"Have you ever considered courting Molly?"

Well. That came out of nowhere.

"Don't look so astonished. She's a wonderful girl. Kind, funny, pretty, and considerably more sensible than either of us."

Emma pauses, then adds with a mischievous smile:

"Although I suspect she'd be quite happy with something less formal than a courtship, if that's more to your taste."

You raise an eyebrow.

"Emma Delamain. Are you trying to arrange my love life?"

"Someone ought to. You seem to be making remarkably little progress on your own."

She laughs, then grows a little more serious.

"Honestly, Antonio, I think she rather likes you. I can't promise anything, of course, but I doubt she'd turn you away if you asked."

She takes another sip of her drink, watching you with genuine curiosity.

"Well? What do you think?"

## Effects

- None.

## Options

### Option A: Consider Molly's charms

**Text:**

"Well, I can't deny that Molly is lovely. And we do get along rather well."

Emma's face brightens.

"Precisely! I knew you weren't entirely hopeless."

"You're enjoying this far too much."

"Of course I am. Two of my favorite people, potentially making each other very happy? It's an excellent prospect."

She raises her glass with the satisfied air of a woman whose arrangements are progressing splendidly.

"Now, I shall leave you to work out the details. I refuse to conduct the entire courtship on your behalf."

You suspect she would rather enjoy doing exactly that.

**Outcomes:**

- Add story tag `antonio_molly_interested`.

### Option B: An impossible quest

**Text:**

You've been cutting the same piece of meat for an unusually long time.

Across the table, Emma's smile begins to fade. Perhaps she's wondering whether she's said something wrong.

Well. No point putting it off any longer.

"Emma... I'm afraid I have a rather unfortunate habit of pursuing hopeless, epic quests."

She raises an eyebrow.

"Oh?"

"You know the sort. A unique and wonderful treasure, guarded by a dragon far too powerful for me."

Emma looks puzzled.

"Antonio, what on earth are you—"

She stops.

For a moment, she simply studies you. Then something changes in her expression.

Your heart is beating so loudly you're half convinced she can hear it.

She understands.

"Antonio..." Her voice is quieter now. "You know, quests of that sort can take a very long time. And sometimes they lead nowhere at all."

"I know."

A faint blush colors her cheeks. Her eyes remain on yours, unusually serious.

Then, very slowly, the corner of her mouth curves upward.

"On the bright side," she says, "if the dragon eventually decides to eat you, I suspect you might rather enjoy it."

You nearly choke on your wine.

Emma laughs, and just like that, the unbearable tension dissolves.

"Really, Fibonacci. You ought to be more careful about the dangers you go looking for."

She returns to her meal, apparently quite composed.

Although, when you look up a moment later, you catch her watching you with a small, thoughtful smile.

**Outcomes:**

- Emma love +3.
- Add story tag `emma_knows_antonio_feelings`.

### Option C: Remain a free adventurer

**Text:**

"Thank you for your concern, Emma, but I think I'll manage my romantic affairs without professional assistance."

"Professional?" Emma looks delighted. "How flattering. I hadn't realized my advice carried such authority."

"Well, you do seem remarkably confident in your matchmaking abilities."

"Naturally. I have an excellent understanding of human nature."

"Then you'll understand that I prefer to make my own mistakes."

She laughs.

"Very well, Antonio. I shall respect your independence."

She takes another sip of wine, considering you over the rim of her glass.

"Although I reserve the right to say _I told you so_ when you make a spectacular mess of things."

"How generous."

"I thought so."

And with that, the subject is settled. At least until Emma discovers another opportunity to improve your life against your will.

**Outcomes:**

- No additional game-state effects.
