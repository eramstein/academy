Text

You find Emma with a book open before her, though for once she seems to have lost interest in its contents.

"Emma, remember that Italian Codex pattern we found at the theater?"

"How could I forget? The only thing that survived the destruction of Neverland with its dignity intact. Apart from me, obviously."

You present the finished codex.

"I made it. For you."

Her teasing smile vanishes. She looks at the artifact, then at you, as though checking for some elaborate joke.

"Antonio... this is a real Italian Codex. You spent your own materials crafting this?"

"I thought its owner ought to be someone who truly loves Italian cards."

Emma runs her fingertips over the binding. Her eyes shine, and she takes a moment longer than usual to find her voice.

"You impossible man." She gives a small, unsteady laugh. "I was perfectly happy knowing you'd have it. You didn't have to do this."

"I know."

That answer seems to touch her more than anything else. She steps forward and embraces you, tightly and without a trace of her usual theatrical composure.

When she draws back, she clears her throat.

"Well. As it happens, I have something for you too. I was going to save it for a suitable occasion, but I suppose you've rather forced my hand."

From between the pages of her book she produces a card, carefully wrapped in tissue. Its illustration shows a tiny, mischievous fairy scattering sparkling chaos across a collapsing theater, while an unfortunate boy in green struggles to keep his balance.

"I made it after our memorable theatrical career. Chaos Faerie. A souvenir of our finest performance."

"Our only performance."

"Which makes it our finest by definition."

You examine the card. The fairy's face is unmistakably Emma's, and the unfortunate boy bears a distressing resemblance to you.

"You made me look ridiculous."

"I worked from life."

You look up to find her smiling at you with a warmth that has nothing to do with teasing.

"Thank you, Antonio. I shall treasure it."

"The codex?"

"That too."
