Opening
Emma approaches you carrying a thick botanical encyclopedia, several folded maps, and the unmistakable expression of someone who has had a very interesting idea.

"Antonio. How would you feel about a little field research?"

Before you can answer, she unfolds a map of the Forest of Schlupak.

"I've been studying a rather remarkable species of mushroom. Schlupak's Thorncap. The flesh contains an extraordinarily potent defensive toxin. Properly distilled, it can be used to strengthen retaliatory enchantments, poisons, even direct damage spells."

She turns a page, revealing an illustration of a rather unpleasant-looking fungus.

"Unfortunately, it grows almost exclusively in the Forest of Schlupak. Which, as you may know, has a somewhat regrettable reputation."

A few days later, after a long walk beneath increasingly ancient trees, you find yourselves overlooking a sunken clearing. Thick clusters of dark crimson mushrooms grow among enormous tangled roots.

Emma kneels to examine the nearest specimens.

"Perfect. Exactly what we need."

She gestures toward a handful of mushrooms growing near the path, then toward a much larger cluster farther down the slope.

"Those near us are easily accessible. The others..." She studies the loose earth and the steep descent. "Well, we'd have to climb down. The ground doesn't look terribly stable."

She closes her notebook.

"Two specimens, safely gathered. Or four, if you're feeling ambitious. Your choice, Antonio."
Options
A — Collect the accessible mushrooms

You gather the two nearest mushrooms while Emma prepares the protective containers. Their dark red caps release a faint, acrid smell as you carefully separate them from the roots.

"An excellent harvest," Emma declares, inspecting the specimens. "Not spectacular, admittedly, but perfectly respectable."

She glances down toward the larger cluster.

"Although I confess, there's something rather irritating about leaving perfectly good ingredients behind."

B — Climb down for the larger harvest

Check: Dexterity — Hard.

On success:

You carefully descend the slope, testing each foothold before committing your weight. Several stones tumble away beneath your boots, but you reach the lower roots safely.

Emma calls down instructions while you collect the mushrooms.

"Mind the red ones! No, not the mushrooms, the roots! The mushrooms are supposed to be red!"

Eventually, you scramble back up with four magnificent specimens.

Emma examines the contents of your bag, plainly delighted.

"Splendid work, Antonio. Four perfect specimens, and all your limbs still attached. A remarkably successful expedition."

On failure:

The first few steps go well. Then a loose root gives way under your boot.

You slide down the slope in a spectacular shower of dirt, leaves, and small stones, coming to rest directly in the middle of the mushroom colony.

There is a distinctly unpleasant squelching sound.

By the time Emma helps you back onto solid ground, the mushrooms are thoroughly crushed and your clothing has acquired a remarkable collection of stains.

She surveys the devastation with scholarly dismay.

"Well. On the positive side, we've confirmed that the mushrooms release their toxins when subjected to physical trauma."

She looks at your ruined trousers.

"Rather enthusiastically, in fact."
