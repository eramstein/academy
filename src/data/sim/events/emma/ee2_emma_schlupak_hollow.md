Manual trigger: Emma has another proposal — Forest of Schlupak

Rewards (choose one):

- 2 Dreamspheres — improve spell control effects.
- 2 SchlupakSpores — improve draw and bounce effects.
  Opening
  "Antonio, do you remember our little expedition to the Forest of Schlupak?"

Emma produces a new map before you can reply.

"I've discovered something rather intriguing. There's an ancient hollow deep in the forest where two exceptionally rare magical substances can be found. I propose we investigate."

She looks at you with a smile.

"And this time, I've brought considerably better equipment."

Your second expedition takes you deeper into Schlupak than before. Eventually, the trees give way to an enormous hollow oak, its interior illuminated by an eerie blue-green light.

Inside, enormous translucent spheres hang suspended among the branches, slowly pulsing with pale violet light. Beneath them, clusters of delicate mushrooms release clouds of iridescent spores.

Emma stops dead.

"Oh, that's magnificent."

She approaches a floating sphere, examining it with obvious fascination.

"Schlupak Dreamspheres. Concentrated oneiric energy! Their magical properties are extraordinary. They're particularly useful for enchantments that manipulate the behavior of other spells and creatures."

Then she notices the mushrooms.

"And below them... Schlupak Spores. Equally valuable, but with very different properties. They enhance magical circulation and displacement. Excellent for drawing cards and returning summoned creatures to their owner's hand."

She begins unpacking her equipment.

"There's a complication, naturally."

She points toward the spheres.

"Disturbing a Dreamsphere causes the surrounding spores to disperse. But harvesting the spores destabilizes the spheres, making them dissolve."

Emma considers the problem for a moment, then shrugs.

"We can safely collect two specimens of either substance. Not both."

She looks at you.

"Well, Antonio? Which would be more useful for your next masterpiece?"
Options
A — Collect the Dreamspheres

You choose the floating spheres.

Emma nods approvingly and produces a curious glass instrument resembling a butterfly net designed by an astronomer.

"Excellent. Carefully, now. They're extremely sensitive to sudden movements."

Under her guidance, you capture two of the shimmering spheres. Their surfaces ripple gently as you lower them into protective containers.

The remaining spheres begin to fade, releasing a soft burst of light that scatters the spores throughout the hollow.

Emma holds up one of your acquisitions.

"Beautiful. Just think of the possibilities! Mesmerizing enemies, manipulating their actions, disrupting entire formations..."

Her expression grows thoughtful.

"Do remind me never to play against you without a suitable counterspell."

Outcomes: Gain 2 Dreamspheres.

B — Collect the Spores

You indicate the clusters of mushrooms beneath the branches.

"The spores? Interesting."

Emma produces a small bellows and two sealed glass vessels.

"Very well. We shall have to be delicate. These things are extraordinarily sensitive to air currents."

She demonstrates a careful technique for gathering the spores without scattering them, then hands you the instrument.

After several minutes of painstaking work, both vessels are filled with shimmering powder.

Above you, the Dreamspheres begin to tremble, then dissolve into a gentle shower of violet sparks.

Emma examines your harvest with satisfaction.

"Excellent. With this much material, you could develop some truly remarkable circulation enchantments. Faster card recovery, magical displacement, perhaps even some interesting combinations."

She seals the vessels.

"And unlike certain other specimens we've encountered in this forest, these have made no attempt whatsoever to poison us."

Outcomes: Gain 2 SchlupakSpores.
