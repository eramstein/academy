Event: A Gift from a Horse

NPC: emma
Event ID: emma_horse_gift
Trigger: Scheduled by emma_schlupak_ride for the following day's evening, only after letting the horse eat the honey or arriving too late to save it.
Emotion: happy

Text

Emma catches up with you in the evening, carrying a card wrapped in a square of clean linen.

"I brought you something," she says. "Before you protest, it isn't a reward for falling off a horse. That would establish a very expensive precedent."

She unfolds the cloth. Inside is a freshly crafted card depicting a sturdy, bright-eyed horse, its mane caught in an enchanted breeze. A little golden glimmer dances at the edge of the illustration.

"I made it this afternoon," she explains. "You were kind to that ridiculous animal when he needed it. I thought you deserved something to remember him by."

You glance at the horse's expression. "He looks rather pleased with himself."

"He ate enough Schlupak honey to purchase a respectable townhouse. He has every reason to be."

She places the card in your hand. Her smile is quieter now. "Thank you for coming with me, Antonio. I enjoyed our afternoon. Even the parts that were objectively disastrous."

Effects

Gift card emma_horse to Antonio.

Emma friendship +1.
