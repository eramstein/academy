  Text
  Emma approaches you after class, carrying a small stack of books and wearing an expression of calculated interest. You've come to recognize that look. It generally means she's had an idea, and there's a reasonable chance it involves making your life more difficult.

"Antonio. I've been giving some thought to our recent matches."

"Have you finally worked out why you keep losing?"

"How curious. I was about to ask you precisely the same question."

She gives you a pleasant smile, then continues before you can mount a defense.

"The fact is, you're becoming a rather interesting opponent. You make unconventional decisions, you spot weaknesses that others overlook, and occasionally, against all reasonable expectations, you even manage to surprise me."

You wait. Surely there's a compliment buried in there somewhere.

"Don't look so pleased. I said occasionally."

She shifts the books under her arm.

"I've been looking for someone to train with regularly. Most of our classmates are either too inexperienced to offer much resistance or too intimidated to point out my mistakes. Neither is particularly useful."

"And you think I'd point out your mistakes?"

"Antonio, I suspect you'd have them engraved on a commemorative plaque if you could afford it."

She laughs softly.

"Which is precisely why I think this could work. We both have something to gain from a proper challenge. I could help you refine your technique, and you could..." She pauses thoughtfully. "Well, you could provide me with the occasional interesting problem."

"How generous."

"I thought so."

She looks at you more directly, her teasing expression giving way to something a little more earnest.

"So, here's my proposal. Thursday evenings, at my room. We practice, compare strategies, experiment with new cards, and do our best to expose every flaw in each other's thinking. No holding back, and no wounded pride."

A familiar spark returns to her eyes.

"Although if you insist on bringing your wounded pride, do try to keep it out of the way. My room isn't terribly large."
Options
Option A: "Thursday evenings. It's a date."
Text:

Emma raises an eyebrow.

"A training appointment, Antonio. I would hate for you to arrive with flowers and discover that I expect you to know how to counter a flying unit."

"Could I bring flowers and a counter?"

"That would demonstrate admirable preparation."

She smiles, evidently amused despite herself.

"Excellent. Thursday evening, then. Bring a good deck, and preferably a few ideas I haven't seen before."

"And if I beat you?"

"Then I shall congratulate you, study precisely how you managed it, and make absolutely certain it doesn't happen again."

She turns to leave, then glances back.

"Oh, and Antonio?"

"Yes?"

"Thank you. It's surprisingly difficult to find someone worth losing to."

For one fleeting moment, her smile is entirely sincere.

"Not that I intend to make a habit of it."
