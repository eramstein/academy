# Event: Handmade Card

**Event ID:** `emma_handmade_card`

**Emotion:** suprise

**Iamge**: medium

**Trigger:** happy_little_accident story tag, Emma present

**Manual trigger:** Show Emma your remarkable card

## Text

You find Emma studying a collection of cards, meticulously arranged across a table. She looks up as you approach.

"Antonio. To what do I owe the pleasure?"

You produce your latest creation, the _Happy Little Accident_, and place it before her.

"Another masterpiece, Fibonacci?" she asks, with a faint smile. "How fortunate that modesty isn't one of your weaknesses."

She picks up the card, apparently intending to give it a cursory inspection. Then she turns it toward the light. Her smile fades into concentration.

"Hmm."

She studies the card more closely, examining the intricate blue enchantments. For a moment, she seems to forget you're there.

"Interesting. Very interesting, actually."

She catches herself, sets the card down, and folds her hands.

"Well. It's certainly not terrible. How did you manage this?"

An excellent question. One you would very much like to know the answer to yourself.

## Effects

- Emma respect +1.
- add to character known traits: Emma is fascinated by unusual crafting techniques and has a particular interest in blue magic.

## Options

### Option A: "I'd like you to have it."

**Text:**

Emma looks from the card to you.

"You're giving it to me?"

For once, she seems genuinely surprised. She glances down at the card again.

"Antonio, this is an exceptional piece of work. You could win matches with it. Quite a few matches, in fact."

You assure her that you know perfectly well what you're offering.

She considers you for a moment, then carefully places the card among her own.

"Very well. I accept. But don't imagine this means I'll go easy on you in our next match."

A small smile appears.

"Especially now that you've been kind enough to improve my deck."

**Outcomes:**

- Gift Emma `happy_little_accident` card
- Emma friendship +2.
- Emma respect +1.
- Story Tag: `emma_handmade_gift`.

### Option B: "Would you like to know how I made it?"

**Text:**

Emma's eyes return immediately to the card.

"I think I can work out the essentials myself, thank you."

She pauses.

"Although... that third enchantment is rather unconventional."

You explain the technique as best you can, carefully omitting certain details involving your elbow and a rather unfortunate jar of pigment. Emma listens intently, occasionally asking a question so precise that you find yourself reconsidering your own explanation.

Eventually, she leans back.

"That's clever. I wouldn't have approached it that way."

She takes a sheet of paper and begins sketching a variation.

"Of course, there's a way to improve it."

Naturally. You would have been disappointed otherwise.

**Outcomes:**

- Emma respect +2.
- Emma friendship +1.
- Teach Emma mezz ability.
- Level up Emma.
- Story Tag: `emma_shared_technique`.

### Option C: "Not bad, is it? Think you can do better?"

**Text:**

Emma looks at you over the edge of the card.

"Oh, so that's why you're here."

She examines it once more, then returns it with exaggerated care.

"I admit, Antonio, you've set a rather interesting standard."

She gathers her books, a mischievous glint in her eyes.

"I was planning a quiet afternoon of study, but apparently I have something more important to do."

"What's that?"

She gives you a sweet smile.

"Improve upon your work, obviously."

**Outcomes:**

- Emma rivalry +2.
- Emma respect +1.
- Tag: `emma_crafting_challenge`.
