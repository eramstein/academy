# Event: The Challenge

**Event ID:** `emma_tourney_kickstart`  
Image: large.
**Trigger:** Emma respect ≥ 5; activity type is class; morning;

## Text

Professor Glützenbaum enters the classroom with a letter in his hand and an expression suggesting that someone has committed a grave offense against civilization. He marches to his lectern, waits for silence, and adjusts his spectacles.

"Students. I have received an invitation from the Royal College of Artificery of Falkenheim. They are organizing a competition for first-year students, in which each participating Academy will send two representatives to demonstrate their talents in card crafting and tournament play."

A murmur of excitement runs through the room. Glützenbaum raises his hand.

"Allow me to read their invitation."

He clears his throat. "'It would give us the greatest pleasure to welcome representatives of your venerable institution, whose dedication to preserving the traditional methods of artificery is widely appreciated.'"

He pauses. His left eyebrow twitches.

"'While we recognize that certain provincial academies may lack access to the most recent advances in our discipline, we firmly believe that exposure to the standards of our leading institutions can only benefit their promising young scholars.'"

The room goes very quiet.

"'We therefore warmly encourage your participation, regardless of the eventual results, in the spirit of academic fellowship and mutual enrichment.'"

Glützenbaum lowers the letter.

"**Regardless of the eventual results.**"

He lets the words hang in the air.

"These pompous, silk-robed, overfunded—" He stops, straightens his spectacles, and takes a breath. "These esteemed colleagues appear to have forgotten that some of the finest artificers in the history of the discipline received their education within these very walls!"

The classroom erupts. Molly, usually so reserved, is on her feet, punching the air with both fists and cheering enthusiastically. Ousmane leans back in his chair, slowly twirling his magnificent moustache with the expression of a general contemplating a particularly satisfying campaign. Farid has produced a small knife and is flipping it expertly between his fingers, apparently considering the matter settled in favor of hostilities.

Someone at the back of the room shouts, "We'll show them!" Several others take up the cry. Glützenbaum makes a token attempt to restore order, but his heart is plainly not in it.

"Quite right!" he thunders, then remembers himself. "That is to say, we shall respond with the utmost dignity, professionalism, and devastating academic superiority!"

He strikes the lectern with his palm. A small cloud of chalk dust rises dramatically.

"Now! Each institution must appoint a captain, who will select a second student. I have given this matter considerable thought, and our captain shall be Miss Emma Delamain."

There is an enthusiastic round of applause. Emma rises gracefully from her seat, accepting the nomination with a composed smile that nevertheless betrays a certain fierce satisfaction.

"Thank you, Professor. I shall endeavor to make the Academy proud."

"I have every confidence that you will, Miss Delamain. And whom would you choose as your partner?"

"Antonio Fibonacci."

She answers without a moment's hesitation. You look up. A few heads turn in your direction.

Glützenbaum nods thoughtfully. "An interesting choice. Are you quite certain?"

"Entirely."

She turns toward you, her expression now wearing that familiar mixture of amusement and challenge.

"Unless, of course, Antonio has suddenly developed an objection to winning."

You have scarcely begun to formulate a response when Glützenbaum claps his hands.

"Excellent! Delamain and Fibonacci shall represent us. You have one week to prepare. I expect discipline, ingenuity, and above all, a performance that will make our distinguished colleagues regret having learned to write letters!"

The room bursts into applause once more. Ousmane rises to shake your hand with great solemnity. Molly beams at you from across the classroom. Farid gives you an approving nod and, mercifully, puts away his knife.

As the students begin to disperse, Emma approaches you with the purposeful stride of someone whose campaign has already begun.

"Tomorrow evening. My room. Bring your best deck, your crafting notes, and any ideas you consider worth defending. We have a great deal of work ahead of us."

"Do I get any say in this?"

"Certainly. You may choose whether to arrive at seven or disappoint me."

She gives you a wicked little smile.

"And Antonio? Do try to get some rest tonight. I intend to be extremely demanding."

With that, she sweeps out of the classroom, leaving you to contemplate the peculiar honor of having been recruited into Emma Delamain's personal war against the Royal College of Falkenheim.
